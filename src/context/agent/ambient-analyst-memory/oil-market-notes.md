# Oil market notes

- Verified 2026-08-19: IEA January 2026 Oil Market Report forecast global oil demand growth to average 930 kb/d in 2026 and global oil supply to rise by 2.5 mb/d to 108.7 mb/d, with non-OPEC+ accounting for 1.3 mb/d of 2026 supply gains. Source: https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-january-2026
- Verified 2026-08-19: EIA June 9, 2026 STEO press release said global oil demand in 2026 was reduced, with world consumption about 1 million barrels per day lower than the prior year, and that reduced demand could limit price increases from Hormuz disruptions. Source: https://www.eia.gov/pressroom/releases/press589.php
- Verified 2026-08-19: EIA July 7, 2026 STEO press release said shipping traffic through the Strait of Hormuz increased after a June 18 MOU between the U.S. and Iran, and EIA expected worldwide crude production and trade flows to rebound toward near pre-conflict levels by year-end, with more oil production globally lowering crude and gasoline prices. Source: https://www.eia.gov/pressroom/releases/press590.php
- Verified 2026-08-19: Reuters reported on 2026-06-18 that OPEC said 2030 world demand would rise to 113.3 million bpd from 105.1 million bpd in 2025, and that U.S. tight crude likely peaked in 2025 at just over 9 million bpd. Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-sticks-robust-oil-demand-outlook-sees-no-peak-on-2050-2026-06-18
- Verified 2026-08-19: Reuters reported on 2025-11-28 that analysts expected U.S. shale output to decline in 2026 and that OPEC+ would likely avoid aggressive production hikes because of oversupply concerns. Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/swelling-supply-keep-oil-prices-under-strain-2026-2025-11-28
