### U.S. Fiscal Trajectory (verified July 2026)
- FY2025 federal budget deficit was about $1.775T, or 5.9% of GDP, per Treasury FY2025 Financial Report and CBO monthly budget review.
- Debt held by the public was about $30.3T at the end of FY2025, or 99% of GDP; Treasury's FY2025 Financial Report projects debt-to-GDP rising to 576% by 2100 under current policy assumptions.
- CBO's Feb. 11, 2026 outlook projects a FY2026 deficit of $1.9T and debt held by the public rising from 101% of GDP in 2026 to 120% in 2036.
- CBO's March 5, 2026 outlook summary says deficits total $20T over 2025-2034 and debt held by the public reaches 116% of GDP over that period.
- Treasury's FY2025 Financial Report estimates the 75-year fiscal gap at 4.7% of GDP; delaying reform raises the required adjustment.
- Interpretation: current US fiscal path is not sustainable on standard debt-stabilization definitions; near-term sustainability is still supported by dollar reserve status and deep Treasury markets, but at the cost of higher interest expense and increasing policy tradeoffs.
